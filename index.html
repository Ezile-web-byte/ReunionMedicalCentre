01 — Who we are

A family practice built for a whole community, not just a single ailment.

Reunion Medical Centre is a multi-disciplinary practice in Isipingo, Durban, anchored by Dr I.M. Shaikh, a registered family physician and chronic disease specialist. Around that core practice, we've brought together a dentist, an optometrist, a physiotherapist and a clinical psychologist, so a family can be seen, diagnosed, treated and followed up without being routed across town.

Our patients are mostly local: families managing long-term conditions like diabetes and hypertension, workers who need an insurance medical done properly the first time, and anyone who has been putting off a dentist or eye test because the referral chain felt like too much admin.

02 — What we do

Five disciplines, one patient file.

General & Family Medicine

Everyday and chronic care

  • Management of HIV/AIDS, diabetes, hypertension, asthma & allergies, obesity
  • HIV counselling, testing & treatment
  • Pregnancy testing, ultrasound & ECG
  • Dispensing of medicines on-site
  • Minor procedures & insurance medicals
Dentistry

Dental care

  • Check-ups, cleaning & fillings
  • Extractions & minor dental procedures
  • Referral for laboratory work
Optometry

Eye care

  • In-house eye tests
  • Prescriptions & frame guidance
  • Follow-up monitoring for chronic eye conditions
Physiotherapy

Movement & recovery

  • Rehabilitation after injury or surgery
  • Chronic pain & mobility management
  • Delivered in partnership with AM Physiotherapists Inc.
Psychology & counselling

Mental health support

  • Individual psychotherapy
  • Psychometric assessments
  • Referral support alongside your medical treatment
Laboratory

On-site testing

  • Full medical exams & treatment
  • Laboratory sample collection
  • Results fed back to your treating doctor
